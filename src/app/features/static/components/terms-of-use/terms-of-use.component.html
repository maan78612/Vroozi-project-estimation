Terms of Use

The ground rules for using ProjectsEstimation.

Last updated: July 2026

Your account

Accounts are created by an administrator and are personal to you. Keep your password private, don't share your sign-in, and let an administrator know right away if you think your account has been compromised.

Acceptable use

  • Use the tool only for your organization's project estimation work.
  • Only view or change data your role gives you access to — don't try to work around those boundaries.
  • Don't extract or share estimation data outside your organization without approval.

Estimates are estimates

The effort ranges and project sizes this tool produces are planning aids derived from the inputs you provide. They are not commitments, quotes, or guarantees of delivery — final scope and timelines are agreed separately.

Data accuracy

You're responsible for the accuracy of what you enter. Changes are attributed to the account that made them.

Availability & changes

We aim to keep the tool available at all times, but maintenance windows and interruptions can happen. These terms may be updated from time to time — significant changes will be announced in the app.
