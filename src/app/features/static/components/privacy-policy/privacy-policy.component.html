Privacy Policy

How Sherlock Holmes Project Estimation handles your information.

Last updated: July 2026

What we collect

Sherlock Holmes Project Estimation stores only what it needs to run project estimations for your organization:

  • Your account details — name, email address, and (for employees) job title and department.
  • Project estimation data — project names, ERP systems, suppliers, interface counts, complexity and risk inputs, and the estimates derived from them.
  • Basic usage records — sign-in times and the changes you make, kept for accountability.

How it's used

Your information is used solely to operate this tool: signing you in, scoping what you can see to your role, and attributing project entries to the people responsible for them. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Administrators can see all projects, employees, and clients.
  • Employees see only the projects assigned to them.
  • Clients see only the project(s) directly assigned to their account.

Storage & security

Data is stored in a managed database over encrypted connections. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed with an industry-standard algorithm before saving. Sessions use signed tokens that expire automatically.

Your rights

You can update your name and password anytime from your profile page. To correct or remove anything else — including deleting your account — contact your administrator or reach us through the Contact Us page.
